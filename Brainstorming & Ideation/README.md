# &#x20;Brainstorming \& Ideation

## Problem Statement

Sales representatives need an efficient way to manage customer relationships and sales processes. Manual order and billing calculations can cause errors, while fragmented customer records delay reporting and communication.

## Target Users

* Sales Representatives
* Managers
* System Administrators
* Business Analysts

## Selected Idea

**Salesforce-based CRM Application for TheChaiShop\&Co**

## Key Pain Points

* Manual customer/order data entry
* Billing calculation errors
* Delayed or incomplete reports
* Multiple registers/spreadsheets
* Lack of timely customer notifications

## Proposed Gains

* Centralized CRM data
* Automated calculations
* Fast reports and dashboards
* Birthday-based customer engagement
